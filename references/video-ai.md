# Video AI Prompting Reference

A video prompt must describe change over time, not merely a beautiful still image.

## Core dimensions

Specify when relevant:
- subject and environment
- starting state
- action/movement
- ending state
- shot size and angle
- camera movement
- lens/look
- lighting and atmosphere
- duration and pacing
- transitions/cuts
- physical motion intensity
- visual style
- continuity requirements
- exclusions

## Camera language

Use concrete motion such as slow dolly-in, handheld tracking, locked-off wide shot, crane upward, orbit, rack focus, or controlled pan only when it contributes to the intended scene.

## Continuity

For multi-shot or image-to-video work, state what must remain consistent: subject identity, clothing, product shape, logo, environment, lighting direction, color palette, or camera axis.

## Image-to-video

Treat the supplied image as the starting visual state. Describe motion and camera behavior while explicitly preserving the visual features that should not drift.

## Commercial/product video

Define the product's required visibility, brand fidelity, hero moments, camera path, background behavior, and final frame. Avoid transformations that deform the product unless intentionally requested.
